Febrile Illness Advisory Expert System

This Expert System specialises in the preliminary differential assessment of common febrile (fever-associated) illnesses — Dengue Fever, Severe Dengue, Malaria, Typhoid Fever, COVID-19, Influenza, the Common Cold, Chikungunya and Leptospirosis.

What problem it solves

Many febrile illnesses share overlapping early symptoms, which makes self-assessment hard. This system guides a user through structured questions and reasons over a documented medical knowledge base to suggest which illness(es) are most consistent with the reported symptoms.

What it provides

One or more suspected condition(s), a matching recommended next action (e.g. seek emergency care, rest and monitor, get tested), and — critically — the complete chain of reasoning behind that conclusion: which facts were used and which rules fired.

Why an Expert System

The domain is naturally rule-based (WHO/CDC diagnostic guidance is written as symptom combinations), benefits from transparent, explainable reasoning rather than a "black box" answer, and has a knowledge base small enough to encode and audit, yet rich enough to demonstrate genuine forward and backward chaining.

⚠ Educational university assignment demo only — NOT a real medical diagnostic tool. Always consult a qualified clinician for real symptoms.

→ Forward Chaining

Data-driven: starts from your reported facts and fires every applicable rule, deriving new intermediate facts until no more rules can fire, arriving at whatever conclusion(s) the facts support.

← Backward Chaining

Goal-driven: pick a specific disease or action to investigate. The engine starts from that goal and works backward, trying to prove it from your reported facts.
